## The experience

Enter your names, choose the mood and let the wheel decide your next little moment. Take turns discovering playful dares, thoughtful questions and unexpected surprises. Build a shared streak, unlock golden moments and close your evening with a cooperative finale.

## Five ways to connect

| Mood | What awaits |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sweet Romance** | Thoughtful gestures, warm compliments and little reasons to smile. |
| **Laugh Together** | Silly impressions, unexpected questions and delightfully ridiculous dares. |
| **Flirty Sparks** | Playful chemistry, butterflies and mutually welcomed affection. |
| **Deep Connection** | Shared memories, meaningful conversations and future dreams. |
| **Mystery Mix** | A little of everything, with rare golden surprises. |

## Built for a memorable game night

- **A wheel worth watching.** Twelve gold-edged segments, smooth acceleration, natural deceleration and satisfying ticks.
- **More than a spin.** Mystery envelopes, double dares, golden challenges and a shared finale after ten rounds.
- **Shared achievements.** Love Points, completion streaks, six collectible badges and an animated Love Meter.
- **Your pace, your choice.** Complete, skip or exchange any challenge without losing points. Affection requires both partners to opt in.
- **Together, wherever you are.** Long-distance settings, saved progress and instant replay.
- **Thoughtful details.** Original sound design, optional music, keyboard controls, reduced motion and responsive touch-friendly layouts.

## Engineering

Built with **JavaScript, HTML5, CSS, SVG and Web Audio**. A modular architecture separates challenge selection, deterministic wheel calculations, game state, rendering, audio and interface behavior. Progress and preferences stay on the device with localStorage.

Verification covers **60,000 wheel-position cases**, **2,500 simulated sessions**, all five modes, scoring, consent filtering and saved-game recovery. Desktop and mobile browser layouts were checked alongside complete gameplay sessions.
